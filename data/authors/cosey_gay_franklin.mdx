Dr. Franklin Cosey-Gay is the Director of the University of Chicago’s
Medicine’s Violence Recovery Program (VRP) which helps trauma
patients and their families by providing everything from wraparound care,
crisis intervention, psychological first aid, social support, case
management and connection to community-based social services. Dr.
Cosey-Gay emphasizes a comprehensive approach to reducing violence,
working in coordination and collaboration with community partners,
researchers and experts in the field.
